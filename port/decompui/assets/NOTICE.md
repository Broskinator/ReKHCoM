the project owner for the launcher cover. Kingdom Hearts and its artwork belong
to Square Enix and Disney. The project's source-code license does not apply to
this image.

`logo.png` (the Kingdom Hearts Re:Chain of Memories logo) and `rekhcom.ico`
(the Kingdom Hearts heart emblem, the launcher's and the game's icon) were
provided by the project owner. They belong to Square Enix and Disney, and the
project's source-code license does not apply to them either.
